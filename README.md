# 🎥 My Favorite Series: *Monster — The Jeffrey Dahmer Story*

## 🧠 Overview
**Monster: The Jeffrey Dahmer Story** is a true-crime drama series released on **Netflix in 2022**.  
It tells the disturbing real-life story of **Jeffrey Dahmer**, one of America’s most infamous serial offenders.  
The series explores his background, crimes, and how the justice system failed to stop him earlier.

## 👥 Main Characters
- **Jeffrey Dahmer** – The main character, portrayed as complex and deeply troubled  
- **Glenda Cleveland** – A neighbor who repeatedly tried to alert authorities  
- **Lionel Dahmer** – Jeffrey’s father who struggles to understand his son’s actions  
- **Shari Dahmer** – Lionel’s wife who supports the family during the investigation

## 🎬 Notable Themes
- The impact of neglect and systemic racism  
- The failure of law enforcement  
- Psychological exploration of human behavior  

## 🏆 Why I Liked It
> “The show was intense but powerful — it showed the importance of awareness, empathy, and justice.”
